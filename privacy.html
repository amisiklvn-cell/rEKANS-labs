Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains how rEKANS Labs handles information when you visit this website, use its features, or contact us.

1. Information you provide

The feedback feature allows you to enter a name, rating, category, and message. It prepares an email addressed to amisiklvn@gmail.com and attempts to open Gmail or an email application. A Gmail browser link is provided as a fallback.

The website does not operate its own feedback-submission server. Information you choose to send is handled by your email provider and the recipient. Do not include passwords or sensitive information in feedback.

2. Browser storage and cookies

The website uses limited browser storage for functional purposes:

  • Local storage: The key rekansLabsWelcomeDate stores the date the welcome screen was last shown.
  • Cookie: A first-party cookie stores the same date as a fallback. It is configured with a maximum age of one year, path set to the website root, and SameSite=Lax.
  • Session storage: The key rekans_feedback_waiting temporarily records whether the feedback workflow is waiting for a return from Gmail.

These mechanisms support website functionality and are not intended to create advertising profiles. You can manage or clear browser storage through your browser settings. Blocking it may affect some website features.

3. Website requests and app catalogue

Your browser requests website pages, scripts, stylesheets, images, and other assets. The app catalogue is loaded from apps/apps.json hosted on this same website.

4. Website hosting

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. GitHub and its infrastructure may process technical information associated with website requests, such as IP addresses, request details, and server or security logs, under its own policies.

Read GitHub's Privacy Statement.

5. Third-party services

The website may direct you to Gmail or other third-party services. Those services operate under their own privacy policies. We do not control how external providers process information.

6. Analytics and advertising

As of the date above, rEKANS Labs does not intentionally operate Google Analytics or Google AdSense integrations on this website. Advertising is not currently enabled.

If advertising, analytics, or other tracking services are introduced, this policy will be reviewed and updated before activation. Relevant third-party data processing and cookie information will be disclosed, and applicable consent requirements will be considered.

7. Data retention and control

The welcome-date value may remain in your browser until overwritten, expired, or cleared. The feedback workflow marker is held in session storage. Emails you send are subject to your email provider's and recipient's retention practices.

8. Children's privacy

This website is not designed to request personal information from children. Contact us if you have concerns about information a child may have sent.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when website features or data practices change. The latest revision date will appear on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact amisiklvn@gmail.com.
